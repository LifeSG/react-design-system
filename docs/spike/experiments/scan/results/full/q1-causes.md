Source: `results/full/analysis.json` (runtime edges with no static edge, and their order
checks) and `results/full/why-missed.txt` (`node docs/spike/experiments/scan/why-missed.mjs`
from the repo root: `rollup/style-edges.js`'s own regexes replayed on each parent class).

fix" says whether a better source scan could find the edge or only a rendered (runtime)
